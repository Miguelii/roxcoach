# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One athlete training for Hyrox and running events, mostly checking the app on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Roxcoach helps an athlete track workouts, manage races and goals, and follow a training plan
informed by their availability and progress.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, minimal, trustworthy. Respect the athlete's time and make coaching guidance clear and
grounded in their training data.

## Anti-references

Chatbot bubbles, AI sparkle iconography, social feeds, competing dashboard widgets, generic
fitness-app gradients, gamification and decorative complexity.

## Design Principles

- Quiet authority through clear hierarchy, spacing and typography.
- Give each screen one primary job.
- Show the data and reasoning behind reports and plans.
- Respect the athlete's decisions without gamification.
- Make empty and error states honest and actionable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, full keyboard access, reduced-motion support and
screen-reader labels for icon-only controls and calendar cells.
